How to Type Pāli Diacritics

Typing Pāli characters (ā, ī, ū, ṃ, ṇ, etc.) is essential for translating exercises correctly. Fortunately, you don't need a special keyboard layout! You can simply hold the Option key on Mac, or the Alt key on Windows, and press a standard letter.
